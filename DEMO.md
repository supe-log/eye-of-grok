# Demo script

1. Open [https://eye-of-grok.vercel.app](https://eye-of-grok.vercel.app) or [http://localhost:3002](http://localhost:3002). Claim a slug, or open the [demo map](http://localhost:3002/u/mine).
2. **Live** → **Copy Bot prompt** → paste it into that account's Chief of Staff (any device once the site is public). The map redraws within 2.5s.
3. Or **Edit** → add Chief of Staff (reports to you), then specialists, then a group space.
4. Click a node to inspect. Mark one **stale** or **hidden**, toggle **Flag stale**.
5. Open **Mermaid** and copy the export if you want it in a chat.
