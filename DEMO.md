# Demo script (90 seconds)

1. Open the site. The messy startup is already on the map: Casey at the top, too many generalists, a 7-person GTM war room, leftover v1 and Q2 spaces.
2. Point at the gauges: bots + spaces vs the 50 cap, over-cap spaces vs the 6-member rule.
3. Turn **Hygiene** on. Deprecated, stale, hidden, and duplicate nodes stay bright. Hidden ≠ paused — Receipts v0 and Inbox v0 still ran this week.
4. Click **Analyze with Grok**. The rail lists keep / hide / merge / close-group with real node ids. Read the onboarding blurb.
5. Open **Mermaid** and flash current vs lean. Open **Connect** and say: this URL (or `/api/mcp`) is what you hand the Chief of Staff.

Do not click delete. There is no delete.
